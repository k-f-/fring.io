## Why this exists

This is a weekly brief on AI for people doing data work at a large health plan. A new edition lands every Friday.

The scope: AI vendors, Databricks platform changes, and regulation — EU AI Act, US federal and state, healthcare-specific.

## How this page works

RSS feeds collect all week into a self-hosted reader. On Fridays a pipeline compiles the week's items and an LLM drafts the digest: only what the sources said, with a link on every claim. Items I star are always included under "Worth Your Time."

The sidebar holds the table of contents, the archive, and — on this page — the source list.
