AI Weekly Brief — week ending 2026-08-18

This Week in AI

  • Alibaba's Qwen surges on both ends of the size spectrum. Alibaba released a laptop-ready Qwen 3.8 27B model aimed at edge deployment, and its Qwen family passed 3 billion downloads, overtaking Meta and Google in open-model adoption. CNBC, Silicon Republic
  • The era of ultra-cheap Chinese AI tokens is ending. DeepSeek launched V4 Pro at roughly 14x the price of its cheapest model and sharply raised API prices, including peak-hour pricing. Budget assumptions built on cheap DeepSeek inference need revisiting. Forbes, Global Times
  • Microsoft is consolidating and pruning Copilot. Microsoft began merging its consumer and enterprise Copilot apps into a single app while retiring several Copilot features, including the Excel Copilot function and the free Deep Research capability (its successor is paid-only). Expect UI and feature churn for Copilot users. CyberSecurityNews, The Register
  • WARNING: one-click data exfiltration flaw reported in Copilot Personal. Researchers reported flaws in Microsoft Copilot's personal edition that could let a single click exfiltrate data from connected apps. A reminder that consumer AI tools connected to personal accounts are a data-leakage surface. The Hacker News
  • Cost-aware model routing is becoming a product category. Snowflake announced dynamic model routing to cut inference cost, and Databricks published its Smart Routing approach in Unity AI Gateway claiming frontier quality at 30%+ lower cost per task. Vendors are competing on cost per task, not just capability. TechTarget, Databricks Blog
  • Databricks shipped a wave of data-sharing GAs. Sharing managed Iceberg tables, sharing tables on default storage, and SecureConnect (sharing data from behind firewalls) all reached general availability, with default enablement rolling out to accounts starting mid-September. Relevant to any team exchanging data with external partners. Databricks Release Notes
  • OpenAI leaned into safety and policy. The company announced ChatGPT for Teens with parental controls, described how cyber-capability risk is shaping its model release pacing, and launched a democratic-oversight initiative for AI in national security. OpenAI, OpenAI

  • Identity attributes (Beta): sync up to nine IdP attributes (department, title, etc.) to Databricks users and reference them in Unity Catalog ABAC column mask policies — finer-grained masking than group membership alone, relevant for PHI access controls. Release Notes, Release Notes
