AI Weekly Brief — week ending 2026-09-26

This Week in AI

  • Microsoft relaunched Copilot as one app. The new Copilot bundles a Home hub, a Code tool, and Autopilot, an agent that keeps working after you close the app. A Managed Runtime adds enterprise code execution inside Microsoft 365. Microsoft, CNBC
  • New frontier models reached the tools this audience uses. Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, and Grok 4.7 all became model options in GitHub Copilot, and Opus 5.5 plus both GPT-6 models landed on Databricks Unity Gateway. Anthropic also pitched Opus 5.5 for Work. GitHub, Databricks docs, Anthropic
  • A cheap "System One" model named Jev dominated the chatter. TypeSafe AI's Jev drew coverage as a faster, lower-cost alternative to OpenAI and Anthropic, plus reports of $10 billion in valuation interest. Databricks already published a walkthrough for running it from SQL. Financial Post, GuruFocus, Databricks blog
  • Databricks made Genie an MCP service, generally available. Any MCP client, including Claude and ChatGPT, can now ground data questions in Genie with Unity Catalog permissions enforced on every request. The old beta endpoint sunsets October 31, 2026. Databricks docs
  • CMS canceled about 315,000 marketplace enrollments in a fraud sweep. The action covers more than 760,000 people and an expected $2.2 billion in returned subsidies, with tighter agent and broker enforcement to follow. CMS
  • DeepSeek hit money and scrutiny at once. Its annualized revenue reportedly reached $1 billion amid price hikes, while Chinese authorities opened a probe of DeepSeek and Moonshot AI over alleged data leaks to the US. National Technology News, ForkLog
  • GitHub Copilot admins got a 28-day clock. A new global default-enablement policy for generally available Copilot features is configurable now and starts affecting user access after 28 days. GitHub

Regulatory Radar

US Federal

  • The FTC is taking public comment on updating its impersonation rule to cover platforms' role in promoting impersonation scams. AI voice and image tools drive much of this fraud, and the outcome will shape platform obligations that touch member-facing channels. FTC
  • The FTC amended its Rules of Practice to reflect its reorganization, including the new American Competition Enforcement Division. Procedural, but it shows where enforcement structure is settling. FTC
  • A White House push for an "AI Force" set off a policy debate as AI agent risks grow. Federal direction on agent oversight remains unsettled, which keeps compliance planning speculative. WWD
  • Top White House tech adviser Ryan Baasch will depart. Turnover adds timing uncertainty to pending federal AI actions. Axios

US States

  • Illinois Governor Pritzker signed an executive order creating a state Artificial Intelligence Cabinet. Expect Illinois agency AI guidance to follow; payers with Illinois Medicaid or exchange business should track it. WPSD Local 6

Healthcare-specific

  • CMS canceled about 315,000 unauthorized Federal Marketplace enrollments covering more than 760,000 people, expecting roughly $2.2 billion in returned subsidies. Issuers should expect enrollment reconciliation work and tighter agent and broker enforcement. CMS press release, CMS fact sheet
  • CMS posted preliminary CY 2027 Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule rates under PAMA, estimating $1 billion in annual savings, and is taking public comment before finalizing. Lab repricing flows straight into claims and encounter processing. CMS press release, CMS fact sheet
  • CMS announced rural health investments in five states: $13 million in South Dakota, $54.6 million in Arkansas, nearly $23 million in Delaware, more than $45 million in Missouri, and $74 million in New Mexico. Funds cover mobile crisis response, maternal care, hospital upgrades, and workforce programs, which can shift network capacity and claims mix in those states. South Dakota, Arkansas, Delaware, Missouri, New Mexico
