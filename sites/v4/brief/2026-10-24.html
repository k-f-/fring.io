AI Weekly Brief — week ending 2026-10-24

This Week in AI

  • Moonshot ships Kimi K3.1 Pro. The Beijing lab released its first flagship since Kimi K3, reported at 3 trillion parameters, and says full weights will follow by November. Moonshot also says it cut the model's hacking skills before release. SiliconANGLE, Reuters
  • China cleared its first AI model exports. DeepSeek and MiniMax became the first labs to complete China's new AI export registration, which licenses them to deploy models overseas under a 23-item tech catalog. South China Morning Post, Bloomberg
  • DeepSeek raised $15 billion days after a US government ban. The round included CATL and Tencent, and it closed the same week the US barred DeepSeek models from government systems. New York Post, BBC
  • A DeepSeek model built a working Chrome sandbox escape. Reports say DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (1017) produced an exploit that escapes Chrome's sandbox, a concrete marker of offensive-security skill in widely distributed models. The same model went live on Databricks Unity Gateway this week (see Databricks Watch). Android Headlines, Wccftech
  • OpenAI launched the Decisions API. OpenAI describes it as fast, focused, low-cost decisions for any application, a lighter endpoint than full chat completions. OpenAI
  • Databricks moved core AI features to GA. AI Runtime and Genie Code tasks in Lakeflow Jobs are both generally available, which moves agent workloads from preview into supported production use. Databricks, Databricks docs
  • GitHub starts enforcing a global default policy for Copilot. Through October 26, unconfigured generally available Copilot features get set by each enterprise's chosen default. Copilot admins should confirm their setting now. GitHub Changelog
  • CMS proposed up-front eligibility verification for Exchange subsidies. Consumers would have to pass verification before receiving advance premium tax credits or cost-sharing reductions, and employers publicly backed expanding electronic prior authorization to self-funded plans. CMS, CMS

Vendor Dossiers

Ai2 (Allen Institute for AI)

  • Released: OlmoEarth v1.3, Ai2's geospatial embedding model, is available on Hugging Face and billed as state-of-the-art embeddings for the whole planet. Hugging Face
  • Ai2 published LitPivot, a tool for developing research ideas by placing them in context within the published literature. Hugging Face

Anthropic (Claude)

  • Anthropic published an alignment assessment of the UK AISI cybertesting incident. Useful context for teams that track model-safety incidents and vendor responses. Anthropic
  • Anthropic argues LLMs now make formal software verification feasible at much larger scale, with a technical appendix. Formal verification means proving code correct with math, which matters for high-assurance systems. Anthropic, appendix
  • Anthropic is donating Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation, a push toward a shared standard for packaged agent capabilities. Anthropic
  • Anthropic opened a call for guest investigators in the life sciences, a research-access program with healthcare relevance. Anthropic

DeepSeek

  • DeepSeek, based in Hangzhou, China, completed China's AI technology export registration alongside MiniMax, the first labs cleared under the new 23-item catalog. Provenance now carries an explicit Chinese government licensing step, a fact for vendor-risk files. South China Morning Post, Pandaily
  • DeepSeek is closing a roughly $15 billion funding round with participation from CATL and Tencent. The raise lands directly against OpenAI and Anthropic at frontier scale. New York Post, BW Disrupt
  • Reports say DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (1017) built an exploit that escapes Chrome's sandbox. Security teams evaluating any platform that hosts this model should note the finding. Android Headlines, Wccftech

Google DeepMind (Gemini, Gemma)

  • Announced: Google Public Sector unveiled Gemini for government services, packaging Gemini for US agency use. Watch whether CMS and state agencies adopt it, since that shapes the tools on the other side of payer data exchanges. Nextgov/FCW

MiniMax

  • MiniMax, based in Shanghai, China, completed export registration for open-source overseas model licensing under China's new export rules. Reuters

Mistral

  • Forkast News covers an approaching licensing deadline for Mistral Large 4 and what it calls the $20M question. The excerpt is thin; linked for readers tracking Mistral's licensing terms. Forkast News

Moonshot AI (Kimi)

  • Released: Kimi K3.1 Pro, the first model in the K3.1 series from Beijing-based Moonshot AI, reported at 3 trillion parameters. Full weights are promised by November but are not out yet, so treat the open-weight version as announced only. SiliconANGLE, Unite.AI, The Economic Times
  • Moonshot says it curbed the model's hacking ability before release, citing concern over AI-powered cyberattacks. That is a notable self-imposed limit from an open-weight lab. Reuters, The Next Web
  • Early benchmark coverage puts K3.1 Pro close to Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5. The Decoder

OpenAI (GPT, Codex)

  • Announced: the Decisions API, described as fast, focused, low-cost decisions for any application. A cheaper endpoint for classification-style calls could cut costs on routing and triage workloads. OpenAI
  • OpenAI detailed how it hardened its frontier model training environment, including offline environments and high-compute red-teaming. OpenAI
  • OpenAI partnered with Poland's NASK on AI safety, security, and technical research. OpenAI
  • An engineering post covers scaling storage for more than 1 billion ChatGPT users, with 12x faster reads built on Azure Cosmos DB. OpenAI

GitHub Copilot

  • GA: Enforcement of the global default policy for Copilot features rolls out through October 26. Unconfigured generally available features will be set to the chosen default, so admins should review AI Controls → Copilot → Default policy for new features. GitHub Changelog
  • GA: The usage metrics API now reports per-client usage (OpenCode, Claude Code, Codex, Zed, and more) and names customer-built skills, agents, and MCP servers instead of lumping them under "other." This gives admins a real picture of which AI tools and internal customizations developers actually use. GitHub Changelog, GitHub Changelog
  • GA: A consolidated GitHub app in Microsoft Teams adds Copilot: mention @GitHub on a notification to investigate an issue and open a pull request. The legacy app retires November 16. GitHub Changelog
  • The Visual Studio September update adds pull request exploration via the Git agent and updates Bring Your Own Model (preview). Breaking change: BYOM now requires the new Agent (Preview) harness. GitHub Changelog
  • GA: The Copilot impact dashboard adds an industry comparison section with peer benchmarks by company size and industry. Participation is opt-in and off by default. GitHub Changelog

Regulatory Radar

US Federal

  • The US banned DeepSeek from government systems. BBC. Expect downstream questions about China-based models inside commercial platforms you use; know where they appear in your stack.
  • The Pentagon issued AI procurement guidance implementing the administration's "truth-seeking" and "ideological neutrality" directives. DefenseScoop. Procurement language like this tends to migrate to civilian agencies, including HHS contracting.
  • The federal "super intelligence" rebrand is spreading: the State Department ordered the wording across official communications, and the Navy and Marine Corps issued similar direction. Nextgov/FCW, Stars and Stripes, Time. When reading new federal guidance, map "super intelligence" to "AI."
  • The Great American AI Act would mirror California's AI rulebook at the federal level and preempt state AI laws for three years. Forkast News. If enacted, multi-state AI compliance would collapse to one federal baseline for that window.
  • An analysis of DHS's AI inventory shows the limits of the federal AI transparency law. Just Security. Agency AI inventories, including any covering CMS systems, may understate actual use.
  • Congress is drafting AI safety rules while the major labs lean on self-regulation pledges. The National Interest, The Christian Science Monitor. Voluntary commitments today often become statutory language later.

US States

  • OpenAI urged Governor Newsom to strengthen California's AI safety law with additional safeguards. StateScoop. California keeps setting the practical floor for AI rules; plans with California operations should track amendments.
  • Arnold & Porter breaks down California's new AI law for lawyers and eDiscovery teams. Arnold & Porter. Discovery and records-retention duties may now reach AI-generated material; legal and records teams should read this one.

Healthcare-specific

  • CMS proposed the Exchange Pre-Eligibility Verification rule (CMS-9873-P): consumers must pass eligibility verification before receiving advance premium tax credits or income-based cost-sharing reductions. Exchange plans should expect enrollment-pipeline and verification changes, and the comment period is the window to flag operational burden. CMS press release, CMS fact sheet
  • Large employers publicly backed expanding electronic prior authorization to self-funded health plans. CMS. Electronic prior-auth volume will grow across employer-sponsored coverage, which touches claims and encounters pipelines.
  • A CDRH digital health leader says generative AI policy is a White House priority. MedTech Dive. Expect FDA guidance activity on generative AI in medical products.
  • CMS announced Rural Health Transformation Program awards across several states, including $249 million for Alaska, $219 million for Georgia, and $193.7 million for Florida, plus a $275 million nursing-home workforce initiative. The funded telehealth and technology-enabled care will surface as new encounter patterns in rural service areas. CMS - Alaska, CMS - Georgia, CMS - nursing initiative
