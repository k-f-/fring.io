Why this exists

Every two weeks I walk a 300-person data organization at a large health plan through what moved in AI. The brief itself lands weekly — news doesn't wait for the meeting, and slides rot in inboxes. A page with an archive doesn't.

The scope is narrow on purpose: AI vendors, Databricks platform changes, and regulation — EU AI Act, US federal and state, healthcare-specific. That's the set that actually sets scope for data work at a payer. If a model gets faster, fine. If a state passes a law about automated decisions in claims, that changes what we build.

Only the public portion lives here. Anything internal stays out, by design.

How this page works

RSS feeds across nine vendor lanes, Databricks release notes, and regulatory sources collect all week into a self-hosted reader. Friday at 3 PM, a pipeline compiles the week's items and an LLM drafts the digest under a strict style guide and one hard rule: only what the sources actually said, with a link on every claim. No inference, no vibes.

Anything I star during the week gets a guaranteed slot under "Worth Your Time" — that part is human, not machine.

New post every Friday. The sidebar holds the table of contents and the full archive.
