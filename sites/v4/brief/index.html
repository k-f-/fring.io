AI Weekly Brief — week ending 2026-08-18

This Week in AI

  • Open-weight leadership shifts to Alibaba. Alibaba shipped Qwen 3.8 27B, a model sized to run on laptops and edge hardware, days after its Qwen family crossed 3 billion downloads — surpassing Meta and Google in open-model downloads. For teams evaluating open models, the center of gravity is increasingly Chinese-origin, which carries procurement and policy questions. CNBC, Bloomberg
  • The era of ultra-cheap Chinese AI tokens is ending. DeepSeek launched V4 Pro at roughly 14x the price of its cheapest model and sharply raised API prices overall, with reports that its top-ranked V4 Flash also underperforms on real agent tasks. Anyone modeling AI run-rate costs on last year's Chinese API pricing should revisit assumptions. Forbes, VentureBeat
  • Cost-aware model routing is becoming a platform feature. Databricks announced Smart Routing in Unity AI Gateway, claiming frontier-level quality at 30%+ lower cost per task, and Snowflake announced dynamic model routing with the same cost pitch. Routing across models by task is emerging as the default way platforms control AI spend. Databricks Blog, TechTarget
  • OpenAI signals it may slow releases over cyber risk. OpenAI said it is strengthening monitoring, alignment, and security safeguards as models approach "cyber-critical" capability, and published a companion piece on how AI is shifting the attacker/defender balance. Relevant to any security team planning for AI-enabled threats against healthcare data. OpenAI, OpenAI
  • Databricks sharing features hit GA in a wave. Managed Iceberg table sharing, SecureConnect (sharing behind firewalls without recipient allowlisting), and sharing tables on default storage all reached general availability, with default enablement rolling out through 2027. Organizations exchanging data with external partners get simpler plumbing — and new networking charges to track. Databricks Release Notes, Databricks Release Notes
  • Healthcare AI procurement moves into the Microsoft Marketplace. Dragon Copilot Physician Apps and Agents are now generally available through Microsoft Marketplace for US customers, adding a standard procurement channel for clinical AI tools. A signal of how quickly clinical documentation AI is being productized. Azure Updates
  • OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Teens. A teen-focused version with built-in protections, healthy-use features, and parental controls — part of a broader pattern of AI vendors building population-specific guardrails ahead of regulation. OpenAI
